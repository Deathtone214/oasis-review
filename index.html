  • All-payer collection: since July 1, 2025, OASIS applies to patients of any pay source unless exempt (under 18, maternity services, or only personal care / chore / housekeeping services).

2. Core conventions

  • Day of assessment = the 24 hours immediately before the visit plus time spent with the patient, unless the item states another look-back.
  • One-clinician convention: one clinician is responsible for the assessment and its accuracy, but may gather input from other agency clinicians, the patient, caregivers, physicians and records.
  • Who may assess: RN when nursing is ordered at SOC. PT or SLP may perform the SOC in a therapy-only case. LPNs and therapy assistants may not perform comprehensive assessments.
  • M-item ADLs/IADLs (M1800–M2030) score the patient's ability to perform safely. Section GG scores usual performance.
  • If ability varies, choose the response that applies more than 50% of the time during the day of assessment.
  • The dash (–) means no information. Use it rarely. In E2, a dash is newly allowed on the D0150 frequency items.

4. Cognition, mood and pain

BIMS (C0200–C0500)

  • Attempt the interview unless the patient is rarely or never understood (C0100).
  • Words to repeat: sock, blue, bed. Temporal orientation: year, month, day of week.
  • Summary score 0–15: 13–15 intact, 8–12 moderately impaired, 0–7 severely impaired. Code 99 if the interview could not be completed.

PHQ-2 to 9 (D0150, D0160)

  • Look-back is the last 2 weeks. Frequency codes: 0 = never or 1 day, 1 = 2–6 days, 2 = 7–11 days, 3 = 12–14 days.
  • If both D0150A2 and D0150B2 are 0 or 1, stop. Otherwise continue to the full PHQ-9.

Pain (J0510–J0530)

  • Look-back is the past 5 days: effect on sleep, interference with therapy, interference with day-to-day activities.

5. Integumentary

StageKey feature
1Intact skin, non-blanchable redness. Not counted in M1306.
2Partial-thickness loss of dermis, shallow open ulcer, red-pink bed, no slough
3Full-thickness tissue loss, fat may show, no exposed bone, tendon or muscle
4Full-thickness loss with exposed bone, tendon or muscle
Unst.Non-removable dressing/device, bed covered by slough or eschar, or deep tissue injury
  • No reverse staging: a healing Stage 3 stays a Stage 3 until healed.
  • A surgically debrided pressure ulcer remains a pressure ulcer. One repaired with a muscle flap or graft becomes a surgical wound.
  • Implanted venous access ports are surgical wounds; PICC lines and ostomies are not.
  • M1330 stasis ulcers come from venous insufficiency only, not arterial disease. Diabetic, arterial and moisture-related lesions are not pressure ulcers.
  • M1342 status: 0 newly epithelialized, 1 fully granulating, 2 early/partial granulation, 3 not healing.

7. Medications

  • M2001 drug regimen review screens for adverse reactions, ineffective therapy, side effects, interactions, duplicate therapy, omissions, dosage errors and non-adherence.
  • M2003 follow-up: contact the physician or allowed practitioner and complete recommended actions by midnight of the next calendar day.
  • N0415 high-risk drug classes: antipsychotic, anticoagulant, antibiotic, opioid, antiplatelet, hypoglycemic (including insulin). Column 2 records whether an indication is noted.
  • M2020 oral meds excludes injectables and IV. M2030 injectables excludes IV and infusion therapy.

8. What changed in OASIS-E2

  • A0810 (Sex) replaces M0069 (Gender).
  • A1255 replaces the A1250 transportation items.
  • Several items were added to the ROC (RFA 03).
  • A dash is now an allowed value for the D0150 frequency items.
  • The COVID-19 vaccine quality measure was dropped from HH QRP reporting with the April 2026 refresh.
